# Launch: useful evidence before reach

Research snapshot: October 29, 2026. Star counts are observations, not proof of a growth mechanism or forecasts for this project.

## What we learned

| Project | Stars at inspection | Observable pattern | Applied here |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| [dark-psychology-skills](https://github.com/YannisKiefer/dark-psychology-skills) | 16 | Clear problem, visual README, one-command installation, original examples, sources; [a curated-list PR](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills/pull/947) merged. | Memorable identity, immediate usable example, source provenance, actual installer check. |
| [grokbot-x](https://github.com/YannisKiefer/grokbot-x) | 15 | Public operator playbook, trajectory logging, validation-gated prompt updates. | Show the learning mechanism and what it changes; retain rejected experiments. |
| [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) | 308,429 | Named outcome, maintained releases, real harness instructions, marketplace distribution. | Make the result clear and publish a versioned, tested release. |
| [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) | 62,319 | Narrow recognizable problem, concrete examples, source grounding, published evaluation limits. | Show actual responses and publish the limits with the evidence. |

These projects serve different purposes. No evidence here establishes that SPECTER is better overall or that any one tactic caused their stars. Their source, branding, benchmark claims, and reputation are not ours to borrow.

## Release actions

The launch consists of a public repository, a usable skill package, source-backed positioning, original artwork, CI, reproducible evaluations, a tagged release, relevant repository topics, a community case invitation, and a focused directory submission where contribution rules permit it. Actual completion and upstream acceptance are separate; see the release and linked launch issue for current receipts.

The promotion angle is concrete: **the same agent should know when to negotiate, apologize, investigate, or simply listen.** Demonstrations should include a price objection, a mistake, and a vulnerable person. Share the relevant example and evaluation limits together.

No star purchases, reciprocal-star schemes, bots posing as users, repetitive self-installs, bulk issues, unrelated promotional replies, or invented testimonials. No recurring external posting automation is installed. Future distribution should follow real user feedback, not a synthetic engagement counter.

## Directory rules matter

[Ezeafk/awesome-agent-skills](https://github.com/Ezeafk/awesome-agent-skills) is an appropriate link-only candidate: its [contribution rules](https://github.com/Ezeafk/awesome-agent-skills/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) allow useful structured capabilities without a star or age minimum and ask for a disclosed quality self-assessment. The entry should claim only the platform actually tested. AI assistance and project ownership must be disclosed.

[Composio's rules](https://github.com/ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) require actual Claude-platform testing; Codex review alone does not meet that requirement. [VoltAgent's current rules](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-agent-skills/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) reject brand-new skills without community usage. [travisvn](https://github.com/travisvn/awesome-claude-skills/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) has a star minimum and prohibits AI-assisted submissions. Prior accepted placements do not waive current rules. Those targets are deferred.

## Feedback loop

Collect de-identified failure cases with the model, harness, prompt, actual answer, and expected property. Reproduce the failure, improve the smallest relevant instruction, and rerun relevant cases plus a fresh independent case. Keep fidelity and practical quality separate. Publish observed results and rejected candidates; do not rewrite the benchmark to make a score look better.

GitHub stars, unique clones, genuine installs, external referrals, and contributed cases can describe reach once observed. They do not prove better decisions. Virality and directory acceptance cannot be guaranteed.
